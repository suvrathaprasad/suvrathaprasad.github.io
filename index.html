WHO I AM

About

Bridging computational biology and evolutionary genomics

I'm a computational biologist with a PhD from Friedrich Schiller Universität Jena, specializing in evolutionary genomics and statistical genetics. My research focuses on understanding sex chromosome evolution through large-scale genomics and transcriptomics analysis.

Over six years of research across Germany and India, I've developed expertise in building reproducible bioinformatics pipelines that process multi-terabyte datasets. My work combines advanced statistical methods, machine learning, and cloud computing to uncover biological insights.

With multiple publications in journals including Genome Biology and Evolution, Molecular Ecology, and Scientific Data, I've contributed to advancing our understanding of dosage compensation, chromosome evolution, and genome assemblies across diverse species.
